Privacy policy: Plagx Draft Preflight for Word

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Plagx Draft Preflight is a free, open-source Microsoft Word add-in by Plagiarism Checker X.

What the add-in accesses

When you click Check draft, the add-in reads the text of the open document (or your selection) inside its task pane. When you click an issue, it selects that text in the document. It never edits your text, and it cannot open other files.

How your data is used

  • The text is checked inside the task pane, in your browser, and discarded when you close it.
  • Nothing is stored, logged or kept, by us or by the add-in.
  • Nothing is sent to Plagiarism Checker X or any other service. The add-in makes no network requests with your content; the only files it loads are its own code from GitHub Pages and Microsoft’s Office.js library.
  • No accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. No data is sold or shared.

Retention and deletion

The add-in keeps no data, so there is nothing to delete. You can remove it at any time from Home → Add-ins → My Add-ins.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/plagiarismcheckerx/plagx-preflight-word/issues.
